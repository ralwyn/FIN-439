### The claim under pressure

Claim: the portfolio is sufficiently stable to use as an adopted scholarship policy.

Challenge: change only the estimation window from all saved returns to the latest thirty-six, hold the mandate and costs fixed, and compare allocations. The largest movement is twenty-five percentage points, above the proposed ten-point committee-review trigger. The calculations can be correct while the policy claim fails. Disposition: qualify the recommendation to a paper research pilot; do not adopt.

**Skeptical question:** why does lowering the equity return assumption increase the equity weight?

**Answer:** the return target stays fixed. More equity is needed to replace the reduced estimated contribution; modeled volatility rises. That is a warning about estimation sensitivity, not proof of a better allocation.

**Skeptical question:** are your gross/net outputs realized backtest results?

**Answer:** no. They are modeled annual return estimates after a stated rebalance-cost calculation. The saved sample and current-designed monitor do not establish an out-of-sample return advantage.
