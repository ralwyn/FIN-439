# Campus Opportunity Scholarship Fund — committee memo

Education, not investment advice.

## Committee ask

Authorize a paper research pilot and defer live adoption. Preserve the 6.5% planning target and minimum 10% BIL reserve. The full-history starting mix is 60% BIL / 40% VT. Across the documented window and estimate stresses, the research envelope is 55–85% BIL and 15–45% VT; IEF and LQD remain zero in those scenarios. This is a scenario range, not evidence that every mix in it is suitable.

## Signal and company screen

The DGS10 monitor preserves archived vintages and actual source-arrival times. Its endpoint change is −30 basis points; the proposed +25-basis-point rate-rise alert does not fire. It has no demonstrated predictive return benefit and does not automatically change weights. The NVIDIA investor panel is supporting due diligence: Buffett and Cathie Wood pass their checks, while Burry flags cash conversion. It does not justify an individual-stock position or alter the ETF universe.

## Conditions, monitoring and reversal

Before adoption, resolve cash-flow timing, loss tolerance, forward-looking return assumptions, realistic execution costs and benchmark acceptance with the committee. The portfolio analyst monitors data timing/coverage and reserve sufficiency monthly and before rebalancing. Refresh stale signal evidence; investigate cash conversion before relying on the company screen. Pause on missing/unverifiable sources, reserve or concentration failure, infeasible net target, or more than ten points of sensitivity. The last condition already prevents adoption today. Reject deployment if these limitations cannot be resolved without weakening the mandate.
