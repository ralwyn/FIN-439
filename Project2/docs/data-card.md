### DGS10 data card and signal rule

Source: Federal Reserve H.15, distributed by FRED/ALFRED. Measure: ten-year constant-maturity Treasury yield; daily, percent per annum, not seasonally adjusted. This is a yield, not IEF total return or a forecast. Missing dates are omitted, not replaced with zero.

Archived vintages: October 5 and October 31, 2026. Latest observations are October 2 at 5.28% and October 29 at 4.98%; difference −30 basis points. Both source files were downloaded November 3 and are preserved with download time, URL and SHA-256. Measurement, vintage and actual download dates are shown separately. For this local system, admit only after the actual saved download plus at least one weekday; November 4 is the earliest displayed admission date. The app rejects an earlier cutoff. Holiday/execution-clock checks remain necessary for a real trade.

Rule set November 10: an increase of at least 25 basis points between these archived endpoints triggers committee rate-risk review. It does not trade or imply excess returns. This saved pair is an illustrative governance monitor; it is not a rolling monthly backtest. New runs must archive new vintages and their actual arrival time. The current state is no rate-rise alert. Quarantine if source timing cannot be supported; require refresh if the latest observation is more than fifteen calendar days old. No alert is not proof of low risk.

H.15 commonly publishes after the equity-market close; an observation date is not an intraday availability timestamp. Current revised data plus an arbitrary lag is not a replacement for vintage history. A zero revision count in one comparison does not guarantee future corrections cannot occur.
