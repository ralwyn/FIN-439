If the test comes back infeasible, the committee has to choose between three things it can't all have: the 6.5% target, a 25% reserve, and the current four-ETF menu — one of them has to give.

The core claim to check against the actual run: raising the BIL floor makes the recommended portfolio riskier, not safer, and may break feasibility entirely.
